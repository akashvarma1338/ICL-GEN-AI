# Professional Email Writing Guidelines and Reference Samples

## 1. General Email Conventions
- Always include a concise, descriptive Subject line that immediately indicates the email's topic and urgency (e.g., "Leave Application: [Dates] - [Your Name]", "Meeting Request: Q3 Project Review").
- Use an appropriate professional greeting: "Dear [Name]", "Dear Mr./Ms./Dr. [Last Name]", or "Good morning/afternoon [Team/Name]".
- Opening sentence: State the purpose of the email directly and courteously without unnecessary preamble.
- Middle paragraphs: Provide clear, factual supporting details in bullet points or short paragraphs.
- Call to Action (CTA): Clearly define what next steps are expected, deadlines, or requested feedback.
- Professional Closing: "Best regards", "Kind regards", "Sincerely", followed by the sender's full name, designation, and contact details.

## 2. Job Application Email Template & Guidelines
- Subject: Application for [Job Title] Role - [Your Full Name]
- Salutation: Dear [Hiring Manager Name / Recruitment Team],
- Purpose: Express genuine interest in the vacancy and state where the opening was found.
- Body: Highlight 2-3 key accomplishments or skills directly relevant to the role. Keep descriptions factual and aligned with the candidate's actual qualifications.
- Closing: Express appreciation for their time, mention attached resume/portfolio, and state availability for an interview.
- Signature: Full name, phone number, LinkedIn profile link.

## 3. Leave Request Email Template & Guidelines
- Subject: Leave Request Application: [Leave Dates] - [Employee Name]
- Salutation: Dear [Manager's Name],
- Purpose: Clearly state the requested duration and specific start and end dates.
- Reason: State the reason (personal emergency, medical appointment, family commitment, planned vacation).
- Work Handover: Explicitly describe arrangements made to cover urgent tasks during the absence. Mention which colleague will assist or act as point of contact.
- Emergency Contact: Confirm whether you will have access to email or phone for urgent matters.
- Closing: Thank the manager for their understanding and support.

## 4. Meeting Request Email Template & Guidelines
- Subject: Meeting Request: [Topic or Objective] - [Proposed Week/Date]
- Salutation: Dear [Colleague/Client Name],
- Purpose: Request a 20-30 minute meeting to discuss the specified agenda.
- Agenda: Outline 2-3 specific talking points so the attendee can prepare.
- Time Slots: Offer 2-3 convenient date/time options, or invite them to share their availability.
- Closing: Look forward to connecting.

## 5. Professional Follow-up Email Template & Guidelines
- Subject: Follow-up regarding [Previous Subject / Interview / Proposal]
- Salutation: Dear [Name],
- Purpose: Politely reference the prior conversation, interview, or sent proposal from [Date].
- Body: Reiterate appreciation and briefly restate the core value or inquiry.
- Closing: Request an update at their earliest convenience and offer to provide any additional information needed.

## 6. Apology and Resolution Email Guidelines
- Subject: Apology regarding [Incident / Delay / Issue]
- Salutation: Dear [Client / Manager Name],
- Acknowledge: Take prompt responsibility for the oversight or delay without defensive excuses.
- Corrective Action: State the concrete action taken to fix the issue and prevent future occurrences.
- Closing: Reiterate commitment to quality and thank them for their patience.
